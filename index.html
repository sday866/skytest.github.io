Welcome to SnipeBot, the exciting game that turns your everyday moments into a thrilling photo hunt!

Features

Gameplay Features:

  • Geo-fenced Play Areas: Define boundaries for gameplay in real-world locations (campuses, neighborhoods, events, etc.).
  • Stealth Scoring: Points awarded for unnoticed photo "snipes"; streaks and bounties enhance strategic goals.
  • Cooldown Mechanic: Temporary inactivity after being "sniped" (e.g., 1-hour cooldown).

Social Features:

  • Groups and Communities: Join specific groups or create your own for localized or themed play.
  • Discord Integration: Enhance communication and coordination among players.
  • Snipes Feed: Post, like, comment, and react to snipes to build engagement.

Dynamic Modes:

  • Assassin Mode: Players have designated targets while being hunted themselves.
  • Bounty System: Players with fewer snipes over time become higher-value targets.

Interactive Options:

  • Live Photos: Include reactions in the sniping moments to capture the excitement.
  • AI Facial Recognition: Optional feature for identifying targets (requires user confirmation to reduce errors).
  • Location Tagging: Players can tag their sniping locations for added fun.

Gamification:

  • Leaderboards: Track individual and group performances.
  • Streaks and Achievements: Reward consistent performance and creative play.
  • Cosmetic Upgrades: Optional feature for character personalization.

Notification System:

  • Alerts and Sounds: Get notified when targeted or sniped.
  • Counter-snipes: React quickly to being targeted for strategic retaliation.

Future Expansion:

  • Mixed Reality (MR) integration.
  • Polling systems for community-driven decisions.
  • Advanced leaderboard options with ultimate rankings.
